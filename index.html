06 · what 0mni never does

      
private is not shady.

      

        
never holds your keysBurners are made in your browser. You sign every transaction in your own wallet.

        
never mixes funds0mni hides pending transactions from bots. It does not launder anything, ever.

        
never hides a dev's bagNo splitting one dev's buys across hidden wallets. Buyers can always see what the dev holds.

        
never pretendsA burner funded from your main is still linked. 0mni tells you instead of hiding it.

08 · faq

      
questions.

      
Short answers. If something here is not true yet, it says so.

      

        
what is 0mni?
A private terminal for launching and trading memecoins on Solana, BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain. It makes fresh launch wallets, checks them for links to you, and sends every launch and trade through a private route so bots cannot see it coming.

        
what is live today?
The opsec engine and the live Solana trenches, through pr1v. The launch and trade terminal ships chain by chain, Solana first. The roadmap above is the order.

        
does 0mni hold my keys or funds?
No. Burners are generated in your browser and you sign everything in your own wallet. 0mni never custodies anything.

        
can it make my wallet fully anonymous?
No tool can. A burner is only as private as the money that funds it. If it comes from your main, the two are linked, and 0mni shows you that before you sign.

        
is this a mixer?
No. 0mni never pools, mixes or moves funds for anyone. It hides pending transactions from bots and tells you what your wallets give away.

        
is 0mni affiliated with Solana, BNB Chain or Robinhood?
No. 0mni is independent. Logos are shown only to say which chains it works with. Not affiliated with pump.fun, four.meme, Pons, Jito or any wallet.

        
is $0MNI an investment?
No. It is a memecoin. Memecoins can go to zero. Nothing here is financial advice.
